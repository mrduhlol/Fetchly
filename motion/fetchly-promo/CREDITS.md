# Fetchly promo credits and source assets

## Original visuals

Fetchly app screens, Apollo icon, Greek artwork, Cinzel/Manrope fonts and README download plaque come from this repository. Product screenshots were captured from the running full Android app. Share-flow UI is a code-derived reconstruction of `ShareDownloadScreen.kt`, disclosed on screen; it is not presented as a live screen recording. The generic ad-gated downloader opening is an illustrative experience, not an identified competitor.

All phone geometry, camera moves, articulated marble hand and procedural smoke are original scene code. No stock video clips, provider video renders or external watermarked video are used. Three.js 0.170.0 is MIT licensed; see assets/THREE-LICENSE.txt. Platform vectors come from the app's existing Simple Icons CC0 sources. Narration is locally generated with Kokoro-82M, voice am_michael.

The real product preview includes Big Buck Bunny by Blender Foundation, licensed CC BY 3.0: https://peach.blender.org/ and https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/. The artwork is shown as the app's public-source media preview, not a newly licensed proprietary stock clip. Include this credit with the published promo description.
