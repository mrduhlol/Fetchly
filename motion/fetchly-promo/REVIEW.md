# Fetchly promo review

## Creative choices and provenance

64 seconds, 1920×1080 at 30 fps. Followed the user's frustration → Fetchly centre fade → real main page → platform pops → share dialog → supporting features → Three.js hand tap → blackout → smoke/GitHub/README button/cursor ending. The earlier result-first and link-relay pitches were not built. Greek styling uses the actual app's bronze palette, artwork and fonts, plus original marble-hand geometry. No stock video clips or external video generators are used; no renderer watermark is present in the inspected frames.

The Android device was blocked by a system ANR, then device support became unavailable. Existing real home and video-quality captures were retained. The source/share sequence is reconstructed from Fetchly's native Kotlin source and the real media preview, and disclosed on screen. It is not a live recording of a social app. This is the weakest proof shot; replace it with a direct social-app share capture once device tooling works. The hand is deliberately stylised marble rather than photoreal skin.

## Visual review

Reviewed the full scene beat sheet, phone-width sheet, all seven cut strips, and six frames across 53.6–54.9 seconds for finger approach/contact/blackout. A Terra low review flagged the missing hand in a midpoint frame and the black end-card entrance: the hand enters later in the shot, which was verified separately; the black beat is intentional and requested. The tap framing was then widened and the marble lighting reduced; its final still was checked before delivery.

Fixed pale text on the light platform/features scenes, overlapping feature headings caused by future fromTo baselines, and low-quality imagery that initially looked too sharp. The final end card holds the exact @mrduhlol / github.com/mrduhlol/Fetchly text and original README APK button before the cursor clicks.

## Measurements and retained warnings

HyperFrames full check passes with zero errors across lint, runtime, layout, motion, contrast and audio. Final MP4 duration, resolution, frame rate, nonempty audio/video streams and decoded frames are checked after rendering. Audio is measured, not listened to by a human or by the agent; no claim of an auditory review is made.

Retained warnings: duplicate static preview image use is intentional; the two preview crops intentionally extend outside their clipping boxes; WebGL ReadPixels warnings indicate software-rendering performance, not broken pixels. Native UI detail and supporting small labels can fall below the 28px reading floor; primary headlines, creator/repo and CTA are larger. Canvas-driven camera/hand motion is visually verified despite the craft classifier reporting stillness. The final repo read hold and requested blackout are deliberate.

## Sound and watermark check

Narration is original local Kokoro audio. Music and SFX are the six original Pixabay MP3s supplied by the user; no earlier HeyGen music is used. Contact SFX peaks are placed one frame after visual actions, based on measured file peaks. Final mix loudness and true peak are measured during output validation. Original audio is retained locally and excluded from the public source repo; credits and publication details are in CREDITS.md.

No stock footage, watermarked clips, provider logos or renderer overlays are included. The intentional Fetchly, platform and GitHub branding is part of the requested story. The preview contains real Big Buck Bunny title art as source media, credited to Blender Foundation in CREDITS.md.
